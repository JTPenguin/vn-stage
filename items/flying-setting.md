# Flying setting in the Adjust window

Add a "flying" setting to the Adjust window. When it is on, the character does not trigger the black shadow bar that appears when a character is too high in the frame. The character keeps its height and position; only the warning bar is suppressed.

Open question: should the setting live on the character (so it follows them into every scene) or on the individual form or preset?
