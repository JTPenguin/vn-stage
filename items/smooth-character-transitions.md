# Smooth transitions when characters move

When something changes a character's size or position, the character slides and scales to its new place instead of jumping. Examples: loading a preset, adding a character, removing a character, or moving a character to another area.

Open question: how long the slide takes, and whether one duration fits every case or a preset can set its own.
