# Apply a form's adjust settings to other forms

Give an easy way to copy one form's Adjust settings to other forms, so a tweak made once does not have to be repeated form by form.

**Design (Justin, 2026-10-30):** a button that applies the current form's settings to one or more other forms. The user picks the target forms in a window.
