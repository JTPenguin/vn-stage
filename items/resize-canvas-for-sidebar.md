# Resize the canvas when the sidebar shows or hides

When a player shows or hides the sidebar, adjust the zoom and position of the canvas to use the space that is available. With the sidebar hidden, the stage can grow to fill the window. With it open, the stage should shrink or shift so nothing sits under the sidebar.

Related: [[character-clustering]] already leaves a gap on the right for the sidebar. This idea covers the zoom and position changing as the sidebar opens and closes.

Open question: should the canvas re-fit on every toggle, or only when the player has not zoomed by hand?
