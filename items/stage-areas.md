# Stage areas instead of fixed left and right

Replace the stage's fixed left and right sides with one or more "areas". The areas spread evenly across the stage. Characters can be dragged between areas, the same way they can be dragged between left and right today. Dropping a character between two areas creates a new area holding just that character. Dropping a character at the far left or far right creates a new area on that edge and adds the character to it.

Open question: the word "area" is a placeholder. Pick a better name when the item is picked up.
